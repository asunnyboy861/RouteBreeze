## Description
Stop wasting time on inefficient routes. RouteBreeze is the smart route optimizer built for delivery drivers, couriers, sales reps, and anyone who visits multiple stops daily.

Add your stops, tap Optimize, and RouteBreeze calculates the fastest route using advanced algorithms. No more guessing the best order or backtracking across town.

WHY ROUTEBREEZE

Smart Route Optimization
Our algorithm finds the shortest path through all your stops, saving you time and fuel every single trip. Just add your destinations and let RouteBreeze do the math.

Interactive Map View
See your entire route on a beautiful map with numbered markers. Tap any stop to view details, check progress, or get directions instantly.

One-Tap Navigation
Jump straight into turn-by-turn navigation with Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze. RouteBreeze plans, your favorite nav app drives.

Stop Progress Tracking
Mark stops as completed as you go. Watch your progress update in real time and stay on schedule all day long.

Priority Stops
Mark important deliveries as high priority so they get scheduled first. Never miss a time-sensitive stop again.

Service Duration
Set how long you plan to spend at each stop. RouteBreeze factors in service time for accurate arrival estimates.

Route Templates
Save your frequent routes as templates. Reuse them anytime without re-entering addresses. Perfect for recurring delivery runs.
